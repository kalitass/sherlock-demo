The problem

Threats move at machine speed. Hunting doesn’t.

In every SOC today

  • Analysts hand-load logs and write a one-off query for every hunt.
  • Hunts are slow, inconsistent, and walk out the door with the analyst.
  • A senior hunter costs a fortune and can’t scale.

And when they try AI…

  • LLMs hallucinate findings that were never in the data.
  • You can’t tell a real detection from a confident guess.
  • So nobody trusts it for a verdict that matters.

The trust gap is the whole problem. Speed is useless if you can’t believe the answer.
